1. The NLP Pipeline ⚙️

Before AI can "think," text must go through a factory line. This is the Standard Preprocessing Pipeline.

1. Cleaning

Remove HTML tags, emojis, and noise.

2. Tokenization

Chop text into individual pieces (words).

3. Normalization

Lowercasing, Stemming, Removing Stop Words.

4. Vectorization

Turn words into numbers (Math).

5. Model

Feed numbers into AI.

2. Core Concepts Explained 🧠

Concept #1: Tokenization ✂️

Definition: Breaking a sentence into smaller units called "tokens".

Computers can't digest a whole paragraph. They eat one word at a time.

Input: "I love AI!"
Tokens: ["I", "love", "AI", "!"]
Concept #2: Stop Words 🗑️

Definition: Words that are grammatically necessary but carry no meaning.

Words like "is", "the", "and", "a" are noise. We remove them to focus on the keywords.

Input: "The dog is running"
Cleaned: "dog", "running"
Concept #3: Stemming vs Lemma 🌱

The Goal: Reducing a word to its base form so "Run", "Running", and "Ran" count as the same thing.

Stemming (Fast & Dumb): Just chops off the end.

"Happily" -> "Happi" (Not a real word)

Lemmatization (Slow & Smart): Uses a dictionary to find the root.

"Better" -> "Good" (Understands meaning)

4. How Text Becomes Math (Vectorization) 🔢

Computers cannot understand words. They only understand numbers. This is how we translate.

Technique A: Bag of Words (BoW)

Imagine a giant list of every English word. We just count how many times each word appears.


Sentence: "AI is cool. AI is fast."

Vector:
AI: 2
is: 2
cool: 1
fast: 1
burger: 0

Problem: It ignores order. "Dog bites Man" and "Man bites Dog" look the same.

Technique B: Word Embeddings (Advanced)

This gives every word a specific coordinate in space.

Words with similar meanings are mathematically close to each other.


King - Man + Woman = Queen

This allows the computer to understand analogies!
